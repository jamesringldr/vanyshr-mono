## What Vanyshr is

Vanyshr is a **household privacy manager**.

One capable adult runs it for the people they add. We show the listing, take the real records down, camouflage what’s left, and put the rest on Violet’s board.

Removal is step one, not the product. We do not win a broker-count war against Optery or Incogni. We do not win “AI assistant” against Serus. We do not win fear-plus-aliases against Cloaked.

---

## Who “you” is

**You** is the capable adult holding the phone. Not a security nerd. Not a victim. The person who would do this for a partner, parent, or kid if it were obvious.

Three doors, one voice:

| Door | They arrive | We give them |
|---|---|---|
| Capable adult (default) | “I’ll handle this if you make it clear.” | Stalk yourself. The listing. Next step. |
| Scare | Already burned (scam, dox, theft). | Same voice. More C on the report. Do not write the whole product as if everyone got wrecked. |
| Shopper | Comparing Incogni / DeleteMe / Optery. | How it works, Ghost Mode, camouflage, Violet’s board, MyCrew. Proof, not a feature matrix. |

Never write to “the privacy-curious shopper” in the hero. Never write to a panicked parent as the default “you.”

## Promise

**Front:** Your household is on lookup sites. We take the real info down.

**Inside:** We remove what’s public, camouflage what’s left so it can’t be trusted, and give you a list for the rest. This is not 100% gone.

Vanysh is the direction. The ghost is the mark. **Ghost Mode is the operation, not the outcome.**

Repeat next to Ghost Mode whenever it could be read as a guarantee:

> Ghost Mode does not mean gone. It means we are working the listings — and you still get a list for the rest.

## Voice

Sharp. Adult. Specific. Slightly dry. The name and the ghost carry personality. Body copy does not try to be clever.

- **Villain:** they harvest, package, and sell households. Nobody asked.
- **Stakes:** being found. Spam, scams, and family exposure are how it shows up.
- **You:** competent. This is a mess someone else made.

Name the data: address, phone, relatives. Do not name the abstraction: digital footprint, attack surface, peace of mind.

**Sounds like us**

> They listed your address next to your relatives and sold it. Anyone can pull it. We take the real records down.

**Does not sound like us**

| Don’t | Why |
|---|---|
| Data parasites. Fight. Your family’s safety. | Cloaked. |
| Sit back and relax. | Undercuts Violet’s board. |
| AI-powered discovery. OSINT. Footprint. | Serus. Talks over the ICP. |
| Prevent identity theft. $10K average loss. | We cannot stand behind it. |
| Become a ghost. 100% gone. | Lies. The board then looks like we failed. |
| You overshared. | Blames the user. Villain is the sale. |

## The problem (A → B → C)

Use this block on the landing, the report, and anywhere someone still thinks “I didn’t put that online.”

### A — How much is out there

**A is your pile, not the industry.**

The scan is the proof. We look the household up the way a stranger would. The report names counts they can feel: sites, phones, addresses, relatives.

Industry scale is a shopper-door footnote, and only if sourced. Do not use “91% of cyberattacks start with exposed personal data.” Do not lead with “4,000+ data brokers.”

**Front door**

> We look you up the way a stranger would. Then we show you the listing.

**Report**

> Here’s what we pulled on you. Sites. Phones. Addresses. Relatives.

### B — How it gets exposed

**One chain. You didn’t post it. They built a listing and sold it.**

Property records, voter files, and other brokers are the raw material. Lookup sites are the storefront. They copy it, attach relatives, phones, and emails, and sell the household as a listing. That is why an address is public even if nobody posted it.

- Front door word: **lookup sites**
- Inside word: **data brokers**
- Do not lead with breaches or the dark web.
- Do not say “you overshared.”
- Public records stay in the story so we don’t pretend we can delete the courthouse. We knock down the resale.

**B in one breath**

> You didn’t put this online. Lookup sites built a listing from public records and other brokers, attached your people, and sold it.

### C — Cost of doing nothing

**Root risk is being found.** Spam, scams, and family exposure are how it shows up. Identity theft is downstream — enough to social-engineer, not a promise we prevent. We are not LifeLock.

Doing nothing does not rot the listing. It gets copied. You stay inventory.

Do not promise “stop spam” or “prevent identity theft.” Do not use unsourced dollar losses. Physical safety / stalking can appear on the scare door (report), not in every headline.

**C in one breath**

> As long as the listing is up, anyone can find the household. That is how the calls get specific, how the texts know your name, and how a stranger gets a map to your people. If you do nothing, it gets copied. Taking it down is how you stop being inventory.

Credit freeze belongs on Violet’s board because C is real and removal is not the whole fix.

| Dark web | — | **Victor stays offstage** until that product is real. |

**Kill in every user-facing string:** Pilot Scan, QuickScan, OSINT, opsec, AI-powered (hero), sit back and relax, data parasites, peace of mind, digital footprint, prevent identity theft, stop all spam, become a ghost, Ghost Mode as a guarantee, 100% gone, obfuscation, security task tracker, stalk your kids, the crew (for Vinnie/Viggo/Violet).

**Camouflage is ship-gated.** The word is locked. It does not go on vanyshr.com as a promise until there is a button. Until then, public Ghost Mode is remove + re-send, with camouflage as “next” only if we say it is next.

Optional shopper-door metaphor, never a feature name: *poison the well.*

## How it works

Four steps. Monitoring is a note inside step 2. Agents are the hands, not a step. MyCrew is who you add, not a step.

1. **Stalk yourself** — see the listing (A).
2. **Take the real records down** — we file the removals. They re-list; we send it again.
3. **Camouflage the rest** — leftover listings get junk so they can’t be trusted. *(ship-gated)*
4. **Violet’s board** — what Ghost Mode cannot do. Credit freeze lives here.

**Landing — how it works**

**Stalk yourself.** We look you up the way a stranger would. You see the listing.

**Ghost Mode takes the real records down.** Vinnie runs it. Viggo works the listings. They copy you back; we send it again.

**Camouflage what’s left.** Leftover data gets junk so it can’t be trusted. Not your bank. Not your credit.

**Violet’s board.** The rest is on you — with instructions. Ghost Mode does not check these off.

Violet’s board has **no subtitle**. The first card on an empty board must say what the screen is, in one sentence, before any Violet personality.

1. Name the task (Credit freeze).
2. One line of why (C, in miniature).
3. The steps.
4. Done / not done.

Ghost Mode does not auto-complete Violet’s board. Say that once on the board, not on every card.

## Decisions made

- Household privacy manager, not a removal SKU with extras.
- One “you” (capable adult), three doors.
- Two-layer promise. Hard to find, not gone.
- Voice is sharp at the sale; stakes are being found; we are not Cloaked.
- A = your pile. B = they built a listing. C = being found / staying inventory.
- CTA is **Stalk yourself**, not QuickScan or Pilot Scan.
- **Ghost Mode** names the work, not the outcome.
- **Camouflage** is the public wedge word; ship-gated.
- Agents are a named cast, not a chatbot. Vinnie first.
- **Violet’s board** is the leftover-work screen; no subtitle.
- **MyCrew** is people you add. Characters are never called a crew.

## Assumptions taken

- Camouflage is not shipped in the app today. Language is locked; live claims wait.
- Dark web is not a front-door product. Victor stays offstage.
- `/quick-scan` and `/pilot-scan` routes can linger; the words cannot.
- Family plan SKU name in marketing is Family & Friends until pricing copy is redesigned.
- Existing visual guidelines (navy, azure, amber-for-risk-only) still apply. Amber is still only for exposure/risk words (**Exposed**, **Found**), never for **Stalk yourself**, **Ghost Mode**, or **MyCrew**.
